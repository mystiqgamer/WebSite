The Real-World Crisis

Three Systemic Traps in the KTU 2024 Scheme

The 120-Point Trap

The Three-Group Rule

KTU raised requirements from 100 to 120 points (yielding 3 credits) and mandated minimum 40 points in EACH of the 3 groups (Social, Cultural, Professional). Students with 120+ points still get their degrees withheld if even one group has <40 points!

The CIE & Supply Risk

Opaque Exam Thresholds

Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) out of 50 scales Series Exams (25), Assignments (15), and Attendance (10). KTU requires minimum 40% in ESA (40/100) and 50% overall. Students study blindly without knowing their safety margin, causing thousands of preventable supplementary exams.

Data Fragmentation

Scattered Syllabus & PYQs

5 distinct engineering branches lack centralized curricula with exact 15-mark module allocations. Students waste weeks searching Telegram channels for repeated exam questions and blueprints.

Predictive Safety Engine

CIE Internal Marks & University Exam Risk Forecast

KTU 2024 Normalization Formula

CIE (50) = ((Series 1 + Series 2) / 100 * 25) + Assignments (15) + Attendance (10)

  • • Series Exam Scale: Average of two 50-mark exams normalized to 25.
  • • Continuous Assessment: Assignments, tutorials, quizzes (15m).
  • • Attendance Credit: Tiered scale (>85% = 10m, 80-84% = 8m, 75-79% = 6m).
  • • University Threshold: Must score at least 40/100 in written exam (ESA) AND 75/150 overall to pass.
